---

## Design Brief (for all promo posters)

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Urgency element | Expiry date OR "Limited time" badge — top-right corner |
| Price comparison | Show original vs. promo price with strikethrough if applicable |
| Trust signals | "No credit card" + "Cancel anytime" must appear near CTA |
| CTA weight | Biggest button on the page — filled brand blue |
| Background | Slightly warmer than standard brand posters — creates excitement |
